Call for Papers

Safe and Defensive Autonomous Driving (SDAD) · ECCV 2026 · Malmö, Sweden · Non-archival workshop track

Important Dates

  • Submission: July 15, 2026
  • Notification: August 8, 2026 August 1, 2026 (AoE) August 3, 2026 (AoE)
  • Camera Ready: August 15, 2026

Submission Rules

  • Papers must use the official ECCV 2026 paper format (LNCS style).
  • Submissions of 7 pages or fewer are treated as short papers; longer submissions (up to 14 pages) are long papers.
  • References are not counted toward the page limit.
  • Paper type is determined automatically by page count, so there is no need to select a track when submitting on OpenReview.
  • Submissions must be anonymous (double-blind).
  • Non-archival track; submissions handled via OpenReview.
  • Accepted papers appear in poster session.
  • Three papers selected for spotlight talks.
  • Two long papers selected for best paper awards.

Review Process

The review process is managed through OpenReview. Each submission receives 2–3 reviews from invited experts, with emergency reviewers reserved to maintain decision timelines.
