About

SDAD (Safe and Defensive Autonomous Driving) is an ECCV 2026 full-day workshop in Malmö on September 9, 2026. We organize around three core problems:

  1. What dangers appear in driving scenes—including latent hazards that are difficult to observe?
  2. How can autonomous vehicles learn defensive driving under uncertainty and long-tail situations?
  3. How can we evaluate defensive driving behaviors beyond trajectory-centric benchmarks?
